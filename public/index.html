About

Lena Dijksma

I'm Lena Dijksma, an 18-year-old trans software development student from the Netherlands. I got into coding almost by accident. A friend pushed me to try web development, and it hooked me almost immediately. I spent a while bouncing between web dev and game dev before eventually dropping the latter and gravitating toward building small terminal applications. There's something I like about the constraint of a command line, no framework to lean on, just you and the problem.

I care about clean design and dependable code in equal measure. I'd rather ship something a little smaller that actually holds together than something flashy that doesn't. That's shown up in most of what I build: a bit of structure, with enough give in it to stay fun to work in.
